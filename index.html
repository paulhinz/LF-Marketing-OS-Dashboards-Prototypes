Why influenced ≠ sourced

A typical closed-member journey (Gold · $120K)
1. LinkedIn ad — "inference" variant FIRST TOUCH
2. NavCon session attendance
3. Two blog reads — narrative hub
4. Nurture email — benefits guide download
5. BD meeting → membership application LAST TOUCH
First-touch credits the ad, last-touch credits BD, linear splits five ways. Sourced counts this member once (LinkedIn). Influenced counts every touch — that's why it runs 2–3× larger and both are tracked.
